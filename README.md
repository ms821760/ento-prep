# Ento Prep

Phone study app for the Seven Lakes FFA Entomology team, built from the Texas FFA 2027–2031 Entomology CDE rules, ID list, characteristic list and exam bank.

- **Insects:** all 166 specimens on the official ID list with photos and the graded characteristics
- **ID Drill:** contest-style specimen identification, name-given practice and flashcards
- **Exam:** 30-question mock exams and practice from the 350-question bank
- **Guide:** scoring, metamorphosis and mouthpart types, orders

Not an official FFA product. Insect photos are from iNaturalist under Creative Commons licenses; each photo is credited in the app with a link to its source.
